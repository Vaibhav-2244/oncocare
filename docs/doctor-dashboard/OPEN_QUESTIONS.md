# Open questions

- How long should doctor-authored records remain visible after a patient revokes
  a link? The proposed default is to retain authored records while removing
  access to live patient data.
- What e-signature and prescription requirements apply for the deployment
  jurisdiction?
- Which administrator or clinical body verifies registration numbers?
- Payments, fees, video providers, SMS/WhatsApp delivery, ABDM integration, and
  drug-interaction checking are out of scope for this dashboard slice.
